# Case 02 — pre-registration: is FCDD's cost more predictable than ordinary repair?

**Frozen before any run of this study.** Committed on 2026-08-07. Nothing below
may be changed after the first run begins; deviations get recorded as dated
amendments with their reason, exactly as case01's A1–A10.

Case01 could not decide this question. Its dispersion contrast (6.23× vs 2.31×)
rested entirely on one defect: excluding bug01, both arms spread identically at
2.31× with near-identical CVs (0.34 / 0.36). Its replication gave the predicted
shape but Fisher *p* = 0.40 against a structural floor of 0.10 for any 3-vs-3
comparison. This study is designed to be decidable before it is run.

## 1. Hypothesis

**H1 (primary).** The per-defect dispersion of repair cost is lower under FCDD
(Arm B) than under ordinary development-plus-review (Arm A).

**H0.** Dispersion is equal.

Direction is pre-specified, but the test is **two-sided** — a result showing
FCDD is *less* predictable is a real outcome and will be reported as such.

**H2 (secondary, mechanistic).** Arm A's cost dispersion is driven by bimodal
*strategy selection* — forking between a minimal fix and a redesign — while Arm
B's strategy is stable.

## 2. Design

- **Defects:** all seven of case01's seeded single-fault variants (bug01–bug07),
  reused unchanged with the sealed answer key intact.
- **Runs:** k = 4 per arm per defect. 7 × 4 × 2 = **56 runs**, 28 matched pairs.
- **Blocking:** defect is a block; the pairing is by defect, not by run index.
- **Model and effort:** identical across arms, fixed for the whole study, no
  mid-study change. Fresh isolated session per run, no network.
- **Order:** run order is randomised across the whole 56-run schedule with a
  seed recorded below, so a drift in service quality cannot align with arm.

**Randomisation seed: 20260807.** Schedule generated by
`tools/case02_schedule.py` and committed before the first run.

## 3. The oracle confound, and how it is spent symmetrically

Case01's §3.2 disclosed that FCDD's contract was built by reading the pristine
engine, so Arm B held a derived oracle Arm A never received. That confound
predicts case01's observed shape with no method effect at all, and it is the
single strongest alternative explanation for any predictability result.

**Arm A therefore receives a cost-matched pristine-derived artefact**: a
characterisation-test suite derived from the pristine engine, built once, to a
budget of **$21.75** — the measured cost of case01's contract. It is built
before any repair run, by an agent with the same pristine access Arm B's
contract author had, and is given to every Arm A run exactly as the contract is
given to every Arm B run.

If the budget cannot buy a usable artefact, that fact is reported and the study
proceeds with the asymmetry **restated, not quietly retained**.

## 4. Primary outcome and test — fixed here, not after seeing data

**Estimator.** Per defect *d* and arm *a*, the dispersion statistic is the
**coefficient of variation of log cost** over that cell's k = 4 runs:

    CV_log(d,a) = sd( ln c_1..c_4 ) / | mean( ln c_1..c_4 ) |

Log scale because cost is positive and right-skewed; case01 watched estimator
choice move its own headline ratio from 2.8× to 4.3×, which is exactly the
freedom this pre-registration removes.

**Test.** Paired across the seven defects, a **two-sided exact permutation test**
on the per-defect difference `CV_log(d,A) − CV_log(d,B)`, permuting the arm
label within each defect (2^7 = 128 assignments, exact enumeration, no sampling).

**Significance:** α = 0.05. The attainable two-sided floor at seven paired
defects is 2/2^7 = **0.016**, so the design can reach significance — the
property case01's 3-vs-3 comparison structurally lacked.

**Reported regardless of outcome:** the seven per-defect CV pairs, the observed
difference, the exact *p*, and a bootstrap CI on the median difference.

## 5. Secondary outcomes

- **H2, strategy:** each fix classified mechanically as *minimal* or *redesign*
  by binary diff against pristine (zero marginal cost, no judgement). Reported
  as a 2×2 with an exact Fisher test. **This is secondary and not the basis of
  any headline claim** — a 2×2 on labels discards magnitude, which is why
  case01's version of it was underpowered.
- **Cost level** (not dispersion): the matched-pair cost ratio, sign test —
  a replication of case01's only solid finding.
- **Quality:** blinded, counterbalanced grading as in case01 §5.3, with the
  packet defects of §4.5 fixed. Under-powered by construction; reported with
  its interval and no claim.

## 6. Stopping rule

**No optional stopping.** All 56 runs execute. No interim analysis, no peeking
at the dispersion statistic before the schedule completes. A run that dies for
infrastructure reasons is re-run at the same cell with the same tag; its partial
cost is recorded and excluded, and every such event is logged.

The analysis script is written and committed **before** the first run and is not
edited afterwards except to fix a defect, which is recorded as an amendment.

## 7. Exclusions

- A run whose gate verdict cannot be computed is excluded and reported, not
  silently dropped.
- Cost is the `modelUsage` total including subagents — case01 found `usage`
  under-counted by up to 82×.
- No cell may be excluded for being an outlier. Case01's entire ambiguity came
  from one influential defect; removing such a defect here would beg the
  question.

## 8. What would falsify H1

A permutation *p* ≥ 0.05, or a median CV difference whose CI spans zero, means
the predictability claim is **not supported at this sample size**, and that is
what will be reported. The claim has now survived one study that could not test
it; it does not get a second pass on a null.
